How to use
  • Right-click a node for its menu (add child, edit text, mark done, description, collapse, delete).
  • Double-click text to edit — Enter for a new line, Ctrl+Enter or click outside to save, Esc to cancel.
  • The number badge = child count; click the badge to expand/collapse. A blue dot means the node has a description.
  • Auto layout tidies positions automatically; Manual lets you drag freely. Ctrl+drag to pan.
  • On touch devices, press and hold a node to open its menu.
  • Use Open Folder on the left to work with local .json files (auto-save, rename, delete).
